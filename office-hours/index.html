Things I can help with

  1. Getting agents to ship, not just type.

    Most teams are stuck at "Claude writes the code." The next step is a closed loop. Agents write the code and prove it works. Fast, reliable CI checks every change. Cautious rules decide what can merge without a person. Production watches for regressions and reverts them on its own. At Arlo I built that software factory, and it lets our very small team ship hundreds of PRs a week. I can help you find your team's next step.

  2. SOC 2 and security, when you actually need them.

    Most founders asking about SOC 2 need a 30-minute conversation more than a 6-month project. I've served as a CISO. I can help you decide whether you need it yet. If you do, I can help you get it done without stalling your roadmap.

  3. Telling real shifts from hype.

    I believe in boring technology. I skipped NoSQL-for-everything, and I never put anything on the blockchain. I did go all in on agents. I can help you sort what's worth adopting now from what you can safely ignore.

  4. Buying a struggling startup, and running lean after.

    Rhino acquired Jetty while I was there. I've scaled a company to 350 people and run it with 60. I know which is harder, and what AI changes about both.
