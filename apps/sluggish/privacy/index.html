Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026.

The app

Sluggish collects no data. It has no analytics, no crash reporting of its own, and no account. It makes no network connections of its own.

  • It reads the list of running processes, memory, disk space and heat on your Mac, only when you open the panel. The result stays on your Mac.
  • If you allow it, it reads the addresses of your open browser tabs to find duplicates. The addresses stay on your Mac.
  • On macOS 26 and later, an Apple model on your Mac writes one line about an unknown process. Nothing leaves your Mac.
  • If you turn on the developer setting for pull request titles, Sluggish runs the GitHub command-line tool on your Mac, which asks GitHub with your own login. This setting is off by default.
  • Your license key is checked on your Mac, with no network call.

The website

This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers. The host keeps normal server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Purchases

Paddle.com sells Sluggish as the merchant of record. Paddle collects your name, email address and payment details to process the order and the taxes, under Paddle's privacy policy. We receive your email address, the country and the order details, and we use them only to give you your license key and to answer your questions.
